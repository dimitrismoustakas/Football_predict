[RESOLVED] ISSUE 1: Pruning Only on Fold 0
------------------------------------------
Status: NOT A BUG - intentional design
Reasoning:
  - Optuna expects monotonic steps; pruning per-fold would cause step collisions
  - Fold 0 is the most recent validation season (due to intentional reversal)
  - Pruning is a gate, not final selection - objective is still mean loss across all folds
  - Most recent fold is best proxy for deployment distribution in time-series CV

[OPEN] ISSUE 3: Phase 4 Scaler Re-fit Discrepancy
-------------------------------------------------
Status: MEDIUM - potential issue
Location: train_final_model(), Phase 4 Step 2
Problem:
  - Step 1: Scaler fit on initial_train_seasons (all CV seasons except last)
  - Step 2: Scaler RE-FIT on all_cv_seasons (includes final_val_season)
  - The optimal epoch count was determined with Step 1's scaler
  - Step 2 uses a different scaler, so "best_epoch" may not be optimal
Options:
  a) Accept the discrepancy (likely minor in practice)
  b) Use Step 1's scaler for Step 2 as well
  c) Re-run early stopping with the new scaler (expensive)

---------------------------------------------------------
Status: MEDIUM - code smell
Location: train_final_model(), line ~590

[OPEN] ISSUE 6: Comparison Metric vs Training Loss Mismatch
-----------------------------------------------------------
Status: LOW - conceptual concern
Location: TASK_CONFIG comparison_metric vs training loss
